# M2c supervised example — 2026-10-29

`examples/supervised_two_workers.exs` starts a supervised owner, hashes two
inputs concurrently on distinct actors, checks both exact digests and stops the
supervisor. Its peer atom is constructed once per example VM and reused across
restarts. The ordinary recovery CI step runs the example in a fresh VM.

Linux/WSL Elixir 1.20.4 / OTP 29: the example passed in 20 fresh VMs. Zig
0.17.0 debug and safe recovery passed 5/5 steps each, including the nine owner
and five process-fault checks, M1/M2b recovery and the example marker. This is
not acceptance of other Elixir/OTP pairs, a production deployment, or a general
worker API. Revert the example, hook and usage guide together.
